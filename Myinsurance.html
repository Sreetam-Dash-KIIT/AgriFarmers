Crop Insurance for a Safer Tomorrow

Your hard work deserves protection. Explore government crop insurance schemes, their benefits, coverage and how to apply — all in one place.

Government Crop Insurance Schemes

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Government-supported crop insurance protection against specified crop losses and risks.

  • Natural calamity protection
  • Protection against notified risks
  • Support for eligible farmers
  • Financial security
Learn More →

Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme(WBCIS)

Insurance support based on specified weather conditions affecting notified crops.

  • Weather risk coverage
  • Suitable for notified crops
  • Weather-based assessment
  • Risk management
Learn More →

Restructured Weather Based Crop Insurance(RWBCI)

Weather-based insurance designed to protect against specified weather-related risks.

  • Weather risk assessment
  • Localized risk coverage
  • Crop-specific protection
  • Financial support
Learn More →

Coconut Palm Insurance Scheme(CPIS)

Insurance support for eligible coconut palm growers against specified natural risks.

  • Protection against specified risks
  • Support for eligible growers
  • Long-term crop security
  • Risk reduction
Learn More →
